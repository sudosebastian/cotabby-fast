# Tabfast UI design brief

Operating principle: design what the content needs, then remove everything that isn't that.

## Reader and job

Primary reader: a person mid-sentence in another app who wants a short, local continuation without leaving the field.

Jobs, ranked:
1. See and accept a suggestion (ghost / Tab).
2. Pause, switch engine, or fix a permission without opening Settings.
3. Find one setting and change it.
4. Learn the product once (onboarding).

What can wait: marketing demos inside Settings, tip-jar prominence, decorative brand chrome.

## Strongest content

The ghost continuation at the caret — host-matching type, muted ink, accept keycap. Every other surface exists to protect or explain that moment.

## Three outcomes for this redesign

1. Daily surfaces (ghost, menu, Settings forms) feel like a precise instrument.
2. One clear identity signature (accent + wordmark placement) without logo wallpaper.
3. Onboarding teaches with the product demo, not with chip clusters and glow.

## Dials by surface

| Surface | Energy | Density | Brand | Structure | Warmth |
|---------|--------|---------|-------|-----------|--------|
| Ghost / field indicator / HUD | quiet | open | low | engineered | functional |
| Menu bar | quiet | standard | low | engineered | functional |
| Settings detail panes | quiet | standard–dense | low | engineered | functional |
| Settings Home | quiet | standard | medium | engineered + search as hero | balanced |
| Onboarding welcome / done | standard | open | medium | balanced (demo is the expression) | balanced |
| Permission reminder | quiet | standard | low | engineered | functional |

## Hierarchy (notice 1 → 2 → 3)

- **Ghost:** suggestion text → accept keycap → (nothing else).
- **Menu:** power/pause state → engine/model/length → Settings/Quit.
- **Home:** search → status (on / engine / permissions) → pane list → metadata links.
- **Welcome:** product demo → title → Get Started.
- **Done:** how to accept → menu bar location → Start Using Tabfast.

## Fixed vs fluid

Fixed: spacing scale, type roles, accent, HUD ink, icon tile, form scaffold.
Fluid: onboarding demo animation, one-time done checkmark, Home search focus moment.

## Reduction rules for this pass

Remove if it fails the decision filter: no shadow-as-hierarchy, no accent wash without a job, no feature chips, no Home showcase, no dual-hue brand gradients, no rounded display type in functional UI, no pink/heart Support styling.
